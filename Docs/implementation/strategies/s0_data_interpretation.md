## 1. Settlement rule

**Provider evidence recorded in every window's Gamma metadata:**

- Rules text: *"…resolve to "Up" if the time-weighted average price (TWAP) of Bitcoin, generated by Chainlink, of the time range specified in the title is greater than or equal to the price at the beginning of that range. Otherwise… "Down"."*
- `resolution_source`: `https://data.chain.link/streams/btc-usd-twap-60s-streams`.
- `cryptoMarketConfig`: `{"id": "btc-5m-twap-60", "twapEnabled": true, "twapLookbackSeconds": 60}`.

**Diagnostic.** The candidate rule is the TWAP-60 tick whose provider source time is the window end, compared with the tick at the window start using `>=`. It agrees with the proxy outcome in **54 of 54** evaluable windows. The same holds when the close tick is taken 1 s earlier or later. No window closed exactly on the threshold, so ties are untested.

The other 288 catalogued windows had either no resolved observation yet (283, mostly the lookahead) or no exact boundary ticks.

**Reading.** The settlement value appears to be the 60-second TWAP stream at the window end, i.e. an average of the underlying over the last 60 seconds, compared with the same stream at the open. That is consistent with the rules text and `twapLookbackSeconds: 60`.

**Open:**

- the exact averaging boundary and weights;
- whether the stream value at the end timestamp is the value used;
- tie handling;
- that this stream is the official resolution feed.

**Consequences:**

- An endpoint diffusion on spot is a **proxy** for this payoff.
- An averaging backend needs the constituent history within the last 60 s, which is not recorded. It stays disabled (plan S3).
- The window M1 replay showed the model consistently below the book's P(Up) in a trending window. That fits a model that ignores the averaging and the lagged K.

## 4. Payout units and outcome mapping

- **Recorded values:** all 7,573 resolution observations have `provider_status = resolved`, `payout_unit = UNKNOWN`, and a payout vector of either `[1000000, 0]` or `[0, 1000000]`.
- **Outcome mapping (verified):** outcome index → instrument comes from `family_attributes.instruments[].outcome_index`, with 0 = Up and 1 = Down in every inspected window.
- **Unit (open):** the 1e6 scale is consistent with CTF payout numerators over a 1e6 denominator, but no provider evidence establishes it.
- **Not changed:** the warehouse normalizer and `resolution_assessment`, which correctly refuse these as qualified labels.
- **Research handling:** `research/strategy_replay/labels.py` derives a **proxy outcome**, method `nonzero_payout_entry_index_v1`. When exactly one entry is positive and the rest are zero, that index wins. Conflicting or split vectors give no label. Results carry `label_type: proxy_outcome`.

## 5. Fees

Recorded schedules:

| Source | Rows | Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| CLOB | 8,044 | `{"e":1,"r":"0.07","to":true}` |
| Gamma | 9,018 | `{"exponent":1,"rate":"0.07","rebateRate":"0.2","takerOnly":true}` |

- **Consistent with the code:** both shapes give the same taker curve φ(p) = 0.07·p(1−p), matching `domain/polymarket/fees.py`.
- **Maker rebate:** the recorded `rebateRate: 0.2` is a rebate-pool parameter and is not modelled.
- **Historical correctness:** not reconstructed. The replay scenarios use the scenario curve with provenance `SCENARIO_ASSUMPTION_NOT_VERIFIED_HISTORICAL_FEES`.

**5b — defect, reproduced and fixed.**

- **Cause:** `Engine.apply_metadata` read only `r`/`e`. In `recorded` mode, a later Gamma-shaped row dropped the curve, so orders were rejected with `recorded_fee_curve_unavailable`.
- **Regression test:** `tests/unit/research/replay/test_engine_fee_shapes.py`. It fails without the fix with exactly that rejection.
- **Fix:** both observed shapes are accepted; any other shape is still not interpreted.

## 8. Required-feed gaps

**Volatility finding (model input, not settlement semantics).** In the inspected window:

- realized 1 s Binance log-return sd is about 2.4e-5;
- 46% of 1 s returns are exactly zero;
- the unfloored EWMA estimate is about 3.7e-5 — upward-biased, because the shared estimator skips near-zero returns;
- the previous research floor of 1e-4 therefore bound every estimate, inflating uncertainty by about 4× and pushing probabilities towards 0.5.

The shared estimator is live code (Z-Gap) and is unchanged. The `settlement_value` research seed is now `sigma_floor: 1e-5`. S2 measures the floor, half-life and scaling (`02_probability_quality.ipynb`).

### Collector clock vs Binance time (found during S2 validation)

- **Observed lead:** Binance trade source timestamps lead the collector's receive time. On 6 October 2026, the median of (receive − source) is about −330 ms, and 93% of prints appear to arrive "before they happened".
- **Consequence in the replay:** raw receive time used as replay "now" made the host's causal pairing reject those prints as future data (`future_binance_rejected`). In 28 of the 37 development windows from that collection, about half of all evaluations were host-skipped. **The S2 vol and grid bundles were produced before the correction below and are biased by it.**
- **Correction:** the replay now applies the warehouse's recorded `clock_observations` (estimated offset vs Binance server time, with uncertainty). Each observation takes effect when received, and the clock is unsynchronized before the first one (`research/strategy_replay/clock_sync.py`).
- **Residual (replay v1, found 15 October 2026):** the remaining rejections were not drift. The collector records two clock sources alternately, and the first correction applied both, with the latest one winning:
  - `binance_time` has millisecond precision;
  - `clob_time` (Polymarket `/time`) returns **whole seconds**, so its offset is off by up to 1 s, while its recorded uncertainty was only half the round trip (about 30 ms).

  Paired samples of the two sources disagree by about 0.6 s on median (up to 1.8 s), so the corrected clock jumped between them. After the correction, 61–78% of Binance prints still looked like future prints in sampled windows. 36–60% of in-window evaluations were host-skipped.
- **Fix (replay v2, `strategy_closed_loop_v2`):**
  - Replay corrects time from `binance_time` samples only, as live does: the lowest-round-trip sample among the last six received within 90 s; uncertainty is round trip / 2 + 1 ms (`clock_sync.CLOCK_POLICY`).
  - `clob_time` is ignored and counted in the run's provenance (`rejected_sources`).
  - The collector now centres `clob_time` offsets on the half second and adds ±0.5 s to their uncertainty (`supervisor.clock_sample`). Raw samples were always kept, so existing recordings need no re-collection.
- **Measured effect:** four development windows from both collections (TWAP 2¢ / 150 s, friction):
  - `future_binance_rejected` 36–59% → 0%;
  - host-skipped in-window seconds 36–60% → 0.7% (the two seconds before the threshold seal);
  - entries changed in two of the four windows. One saved +$6.05 win became an unfilled request.
- **Consequence:** every S2 result produced with replay v1 (`validation_seed`, the staged experiments created before 15 October) evaluated the strategy in only a fraction of seconds. Their entries, fills and P&L are not comparable with v2 and must be rerun as new experiments. Staged experiments record their replay version and refuse to mix versions.
- **Classification:** clock-sync quality (replay reading of a correctly recorded source). **Live:** `OsMonitorClockSyncProvider` already uses Binance time only, so it should not show this gap; signal-only observation reports host-skip reasons directly (`decisions.jsonl`).
